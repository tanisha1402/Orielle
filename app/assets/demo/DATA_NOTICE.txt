EARTH RISK CHAIN - demo assets
================================
All numbers, dates, geometries and infrastructure counts shown by the app in
mock mode are SAMPLE DATA defined in lib/data/mock/mock_data.dart.
They are illustrative and are NOT satellite-derived measurements.
This folder is intentionally almost empty; it is declared in pubspec.yaml so that
future demo files (for example a GeoJSON sample) can be added without
changing the build configuration.
